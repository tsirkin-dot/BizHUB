The four layers

A document page answers “what is this and what goes in it”. A situation page answers “this just happened to me”. An audience page answers “who am I and what do I need”. A glossary page answers “what does this word mean”. Four different questions, so four different pages — never one page trying to hold all four.

These pages explain how residential rental documents normally work in the United States so you can describe your own situation accurately. Landlord–tenant law is set by each state, and often by the city, so a rule described here as usual may not be the rule where your property is.
